# Michael Gulden

**Fractional Chief AI Officer · AI Systems Engineer · Founder** — Meridian, ID

I build multi-agent systems that run real operations. Full-time in AI
engineering since November 2025, on top of 13 years of web development,
UX/UI, and end-to-end product delivery (since 2013). Through
[BeyondSaaS.ai](https://beyondsaas.ai) I help companies decompose their
processes and rebuild them as agentic workflows — in-VPC, cloud, and
hybrid. Everything runs on self-hosted AI infrastructure I designed
and operate: a Proxmox cluster with an RTX 3090 GPU pool, vLLM
inference serving, 40GbE networking with RDMA, and k3s.
